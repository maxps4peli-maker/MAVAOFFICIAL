02 / Approach

MAVA is small by design. Every project is handled directly by the two of us — no account managers, no hand-offs, no layers between you and the people actually building your site.

We adapt to how you like to work. Some clients want weekly check-ins, others want to leave us alone and see the result — either way, we stay flexible and easy to reach throughout the project.

We care about clean, fast, well-built websites — and about being upfront on price and timeline from the first conversation, so there are no surprises along the way.

05 — Process

How a project runs

Four steps, start to finish — clear, close, and without surprises.

01

Discovery

We start with a conversation — your goals, your customers, and what the site actually needs to do. No forms, no account managers; a direct talk with the people building it.

02

Design

We shape the direction — layout, typography and visual identity — and share it early, so you can react before a single line of code is written.

03

Build

We build the site by hand — fast, responsive and carefully finished — keeping you in the loop at whatever pace suits you.

04

Launch

We launch, test everything on real devices, and hand over a site that's genuinely yours to run — and we're still here after go-live.
